02Hukumu kwa ufupi

SokoHai ina Business OS ya kweli nyuma ya pazia — inventory authority, POS ya server-side, escrow, reservations, ripoti — lakini haijasomeshwa kama mfumo mmoja. Kuna injini nzuri nyingi zinazoendesha dashboards sita tofauti, na sehemu muhimu za Business OS hazipo kabisa.

11zinafanya kazi

Injini halisi + rules + tests

12partial

Zipo, zinatumika, hazijakamilika

4jina tu

Collection/field/tab tupu au iliyokufa

3 + 6hazipo / zimegawanyika

3 hazipo kabisa, 6 zimegawika

Sentensi moja Injini za nyuma (inventory, POS, escrow, reservations, reports) ni za kiwango cha juu; shell ya mbele (dashboard, navigation, capabilities, roles) ndiyo kiungo dhaifu — na ndiyo hasa kitu unachoomba kujengwa.
Hatari tatu za haraka
  1. POS level ni mapambo: sokohai_pos_level inaishi kwenye localStorage; hakuna UI, hakuna server enforcement. Mtu yeyote anaweza kuweka full kwa console.
  2. Ledger mbili za mauzo: sales (server-only, authoritative) na shop_ledger (client-writable) zinaandikwa kwa muamala mmoja; dashboards zinasoma shop_ledger.
  3. Staff = owner: ownsShopRecord() inampa kila staff aliyelink haki zote za mmiliki — hakuna role granularity server-side.

07D · Architecture Diagram

Core identity (server-authoritative) Sales channels Money & fulfilment Mtiririko wa pesa

10POS Control Levels × Selling Mode

  • ✘ inventory · purchases · customers · suppliers · expenses · reports · staff · outlets

FULL
