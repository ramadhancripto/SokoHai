SokoHai works in your browser and can be installed on your phone from the browser menu (“Add to Home screen”). You can browse without an account; to chat, sell or save items, create a free account.

Account and login

How do I create an account?
Open SokoHai, tap Ingia, then Jisajili hapa. Enter your name, e-mail, phone number and a password of at least 6 characters, or continue with Google.
I forgot my password
On the sign-in screen tap Ingia kwa Barua Pepe, type your e-mail, then Umesahau nenosiri?. You will receive a link to set a new password (check Spam too). For safety the message is the same whether or not the e-mail has an account.
Can I sign in with a phone number?
Not yet. Sign in with e-mail and password, or with Google.
How do I change my profile or photo?
Open the menu ☰ → your profile. You can change your photo, cover and shop details there.
How do I close my account?
Send a request through Contact Support and choose “Faragha na data yangu”. A self-service delete button is not available yet.

Buying on SokoHai

How do I buy something?
Open the listing, tap to chat with the seller, and agree the price, payment and hand-over. SokoHai does not process payments for sales yet, so you pay the seller directly.
How do I stay safe?
  • Keep the conversation in SokoHai chat.
  • Inspect the item before paying where possible.
  • Be careful with advance payments to unknown accounts or prices that look too good.
  • SokoHai will never ask for your password, PIN or one-time code.
How do I report a listing?
Open the listing and tap Ripoti, choose a reason and send. The SokoHai team reviews reports and can hide listings.

Selling on SokoHai

How much does it cost to sell?
Listing is free. There is no commission on sales at this time.
How do I post a listing?
Tap the + (Chapisha) button, choose the type, add photos, a clear title, price, condition and location, then publish. Read the Seller policy first.
How do I remove or restore a listing?
In your dashboard move it to the Recycle Bin; it disappears from the market. You can restore it later or delete it permanently from the Recycle Bin.
Will buyers see my phone number?
Only if you switch on “Onyesha namba yangu ya simu kwa wanunuzi” in your profile. Otherwise buyers contact you by chat.
Why does my stock show as pending?
Opening stock is confirmed by SokoHai’s server functions, which are being switched on in stages. Your listing is still visible.
Can I promote my listing?
Paid promotion is prepared but not switched on yet. When it opens, prices and payment instructions will be shown in the app.

Orders and cancellations

In-app orders and checkout are not available yet. A sale is an agreement between you and the other user, and it can be cancelled by agreement. Sellers should archive listings that are no longer available. See the Refunds, cancellations & disputes policy.

Payments and SokoPay

SokoPay (in-app payments, escrow and wallet) is not available yet. SokoHai does not hold or transfer money for sales today. Do not send money to anyone claiming to be “SokoPay” or SokoHai staff. If someone asks you to, report it through Contact Support.

Refunds and disputes

Because SokoHai does not process payments yet, refunds are between the buyer and the seller. If a seller misleads you or a listing is fraudulent, report the listing and send details through Contact Support. SokoHai can hide listings and restrict accounts; it cannot reverse a payment it did not process. If you were defrauded, also report it to the police.

Delivery and transport

You can find and contact transport providers under Usafiri. Booking, driver assignment, tracking and transport payments inside SokoHai are not available yet, so arrangements are made directly with the provider.

Live and auctions

Stories (Hali) and live sessions are being switched on; when available you can report them from their menu. Auctions, price drop and group buying are not available yet.

Security and suspicious activity

I think my account was accessed by someone else
Reset your password immediately (sign-in screen → Umesahau nenosiri?) and send a request through Contact Support.
I received a suspicious message
Do not share codes or pay. Report the chat from the chat menu, and report the listing if there is one.
I found a security problem in SokoHai
Please report it privately through Contact Support with steps to reproduce. Do not test on other people’s accounts or data.

Privacy and your data

Read the Privacy Policy. To see, correct or delete your information, or to close your account, send a request through Contact Support and choose “Faragha na data yangu”. You can control whether your phone number is public in your profile.

Technical problems and feedback

Try refreshing the page or updating the installed app. If the problem continues, send a request through Report a Problem describing what you did and what you saw (please do not include passwords). Ideas and suggestions are welcome through Feedback.

Contact Support

Support requests are sent from inside the app so that we know which account they come from and can reply there.

Send a support requestMy requests

You will get a reference number (for example SR-ABCD2345) and can follow the status and reply under “Maombi yangu”. If you are not signed in, you will be asked to sign in first. Other contact options are on Contact & legal requests.
