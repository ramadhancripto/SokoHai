Cookies & device storage

SokoHai does not use advertising or analytics cookies. This page explains what the app stores on your device and why.

Summary

The SokoHai app itself sets no cookies. Like most web apps, it stores some information in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB so that it can keep you signed in, remember your settings and work offline. None of it is used for advertising, and no third-party analytics or advertising trackers are used.

Third parties

When the app loads maps, video embeds or uploaded images, those providers (for example OpenStreetMap, Esri, YouTube, Vimeo or Cloudinary) receive standard connection data and may set their own cookies under their own policies. The hosting provider (Netlify) may also use technical cookies to deliver the site. See the Privacy Policy for the list of providers.

Your choices

You can clear stored data in your browser settings (site data for sokohai.com). This signs you out and removes your local settings, cart and history on that device. You can also block location access or notifications at any time in your browser.

Changes

If SokoHai starts using cookies or similar technologies for other purposes, such as analytics, we will update this page first and ask for consent where the law requires it.
