Support

The fastest way to reach the SokoHai team is a support request inside the app. You receive a reference number and the reply appears in the app.

Send a support requestHelp Center

Privacy requests

To access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or close your account, send a request through Contact Support → “Faragha na data yangu”. We may ask you to confirm your identity. See the Privacy Policy.

Complaints and reports

Report a listing with Ripoti on the listing, and report chats, stories or live sessions from their menu. For other complaints use Contact Support → “Marejesho na migogoro”.

Security issues

Report suspected fraud, account takeover or a security weakness privately through Contact Support → “Usalama”.
