How sales work today

SokoHai does not process payments for sales at this time. Checkout, SokoPay escrow, wallet and delivery booking are not available yet. When you buy from a seller on SokoHai, you agree the price, payment method and hand-over directly with the seller, and you pay the seller directly.

This means SokoHai cannot refund, reverse or hold a payment it did not process.

Before you pay

  • Use SokoHai chat so there is a record of what you agreed.
  • Inspect the item or confirm the service before paying, where possible.
  • Be careful with requests to pay in advance to unknown accounts, prices that look too good, or pressure to move off SokoHai.
  • SokoHai staff will never ask for your password, PIN or one-time code.

Cancellations

A sale can be cancelled by agreement between the buyer and the seller. Sellers should update or archive a listing that is no longer available. Repeated cancellations or no-shows can be reported.

Refunds and returns

Refunds and returns are a matter between the buyer and the seller, subject to the seller’s own terms and to consumer-protection law. Sellers should state any return conditions clearly in the listing and honour them.

If something goes wrong

  1. Contact the seller in SokoHai chat and try to resolve it.
  2. If the listing is misleading, fraudulent or prohibited, use Ripoti on the listing. Reports are reviewed by the SokoHai team, which can hide the listing.
  3. If a user is abusive or fraudulent in chat, report the chat.
  4. For anything else, send a request through Contact Support → “Marejesho na migogoro” with the listing ID and what happened.
  5. If you have been defrauded, also report it to the police. We will cooperate with lawful requests.

What SokoHai can do

  • Review reports and hide or remove listings.
  • Limit, suspend or close accounts that break the rules.
  • Keep records of reports and decisions and share them with authorities when the law requires.

SokoHai cannot force a seller to refund, and does not compensate losses from sales between users.

Paid promotions

Paid promotions are not switched on yet. When they are, a promotion is activated only after its payment is verified, and the rules for cancelling or refunding a promotion will be published here before launch. PENDING OWNER CONFIRMATION: promotion refund rules

When in-app payments are switched on

When checkout and SokoPay are switched on, this policy will be updated before launch to describe how orders, payment holding, delivery confirmation, cancellations, refunds and disputes work inside SokoHai.
