Who can sell

Anyone with a SokoHai account who accepts the Terms can post listings for free. Business sellers must comply with laws that apply to their business, including registration, licensing and tax. LEGAL REVIEW: seller registration and licensing obligations for traders

Listing requirements

  • List only items or services you own or are authorised to sell, and that are lawful in Tanzania.
  • Use your own photos or photos you have the right to use, showing the actual item.
  • Write an accurate title and description, including condition (new or used), defects and important limits.
  • Choose the correct category.
  • Show the real price in Tanzanian shillings. Do not use fake discounts or misleading prices.
  • Keep stock and availability up to date. Move sold or unavailable items to the Recycle Bin.
  • Do not post the same item many times (duplicates), and do not put contact details or external payment instructions inside photos to get around SokoHai.

Prohibited items and services

Do not list:

  • anything illegal to sell or possess in Tanzania, including illegal drugs, stolen goods and counterfeit products;
  • weapons, ammunition and explosives;
  • protected wildlife and wildlife products;
  • prescription medicines or medical devices without the required authorisation;
  • government documents, identity documents, personal data or accounts;
  • adult sexual content or services;
  • hazardous materials;
  • items that infringe trademarks or copyright;
  • financial schemes, “investment” offers, pyramid schemes or lotteries;
  • anything else listed in the Community guidelines.

LEGAL REVIEW: list of regulated products that need a permit or licence

Dealing with buyers

  • Answer buyers honestly and promptly through SokoHai chat.
  • Honour the price and terms you agree. State any return or warranty terms in the listing.
  • Do not ask buyers for passwords, PINs or one-time codes, and do not pressure them to pay in advance to unrelated accounts.
  • Respect buyers’ privacy: use their details only for the sale.

Contact details and privacy

Your phone number is not shown on your public seller page unless you switch on “Onyesha namba yangu ya simu kwa wanunuzi” in your profile. Buyers can always reach you through SokoHai chat.

Fees

Listing is free. SokoHai does not charge a commission on sales at this time. Paid promotions are optional and not switched on yet. PENDING OWNER CONFIRMATION: promotion prices

Verification badges

A verification badge appears only after SokoHai has reviewed the documents you submitted. Sellers cannot set badges, stock verification or inspection status themselves.

Complaints and enforcement

Buyers can report listings. SokoHai may hide or remove listings, limit features, or suspend accounts for breaking this policy, with the reason where possible. You can ask for a review through Contact Support.
