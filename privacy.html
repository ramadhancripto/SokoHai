Who we are

SokoHai is an online marketplace for Tanzania, available at sokohai.com and as an installable web app. In this policy “SokoHai”, “we” and “us” mean the operator of the platform: PENDING OWNER CONFIRMATION: registered company name, BRELA registration number and TIN, PENDING OWNER CONFIRMATION: registered address.

PENDING OWNER CONFIRMATION: registration status with the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) and the name or contact of the Data Protection Officer

What this policy covers

This policy covers the SokoHai web app, the public pages on sokohai.com, and the communications we send you through the app. It does not cover websites or services of other companies that you reach through a link on SokoHai.

At this stage SokoHai works as a free listing marketplace: buyers find products, services and transport providers and contact sellers through SokoHai. In-app checkout, SokoPay escrow, wallet, auctions, price drop, group buying and delivery booking are built but not switched on. This policy describes what we process today and tells you where processing will change when those features are switched on.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: full name, e-mail address, phone number, password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password), an optional agent code, and the date you joined. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name, e-mail address and profile photo.
  • Profile: profile photo, cover image, shop or business name, short description, and region, district or city if you add them.
  • Listings: titles, descriptions, prices, photos, category, condition, stock information and the location text you enter for products, services or transport.
  • Messages and content: chat messages, files, photos and voice notes you send, comments, reviews, likes, follows, saved items, stories (Hali) and live sessions.
  • Reports and support requests: what you write when you report a listing, chat, story or live session, or when you contact support.
  • Identity or business verification (optional): if you ask to be verified, an image of an identity or business document (for example a national ID, passport, driving licence, business licence or TIN certificate) and the last four characters of its number.
  • Payout details (optional): if you save a payment account in the app, the mobile-money number, bank account name and number, or the brand, last four digits and expiry of a card. The card security code (CVV) is never stored.
  • Business tools (optional): if you use the shop and point-of-sale tools, the sales, stock, supplier, staff and customer records you enter. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to enter other people’s details there.

Information collected when you use SokoHai

  • Location: if you allow location access in your browser, your device location is used to show nearby listings and to fill in your area. When you publish a listing, SokoHai stores only an approximate position (rounded to about 1 km), not your exact location.
  • Usage: listing views, searches and recently viewed items (some kept only on your device), and interactions used to rank listings.
  • Technical: when the app reports an error it records the page, app version and browser type. Our hosting and cloud providers also receive standard technical data such as IP address and browser type when your device connects to them.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a push token for your browser.

What other people can see

  • Anyone, without signing in: your listings (including their photos, price, description, location text and approximate area), your public profile card (name, photo, shop name, description, region) and public comments and reviews.
  • Your phone number is not shown publicly unless you switch on “Onyesha namba yangu ya simu kwa wanunuzi” in your profile. You can switch it off at any time; buyers can still reach you through SokoHai chat.
  • Your e-mail address, payout details, verification documents and support requests are visible only to you and to authorised SokoHai administrators.
  • Chat messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Files sent in chat are stored with our media provider and can be opened by anyone who has the file link.

How we protect information

  • Access to data is controlled by database security rules: private profile data, payout details, verification records and support requests can be read only by the person concerned and authorised administrators.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored by SokoHai.
  • Connections to SokoHai use HTTPS.
  • Administrative actions such as hiding a listing are recorded in an audit log.

No online service is completely secure. If we become aware of a security breach affecting personal information, we will act on it and notify the PDPC and affected people where the law requires.

How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes above, including resolving disputes, preventing fraud and meeting legal obligations.

  • When you move a listing to the Recycle Bin or delete it permanently, it is removed from the marketplace, but a record is kept so that past orders, reports and stock history remain accurate.
  • Moderation and audit records are kept to show how decisions were made.
  • Information stored only on your device (for example recent searches) stays until you clear it in your browser.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete information, or close your account;
  • stop or restrict certain processing, or object to it;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on consent (for example, showing your phone number publicly).

You can change much of your information yourself in your profile and listings. For anything else, send a request through Contact Support → “Faragha na data yangu” in the app, or see Contact & legal requests. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. Account closure is handled through a support request; there is not yet a self-service “delete account” button.

You also have the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania.

Cookies and device storage

SokoHai does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It stores some information in your browser so the app works, such as your sign-in session and language. See Cookies & device storage.

When payments are switched on

When SokoPay, checkout, wallet or delivery booking are switched on, SokoHai will process additional information such as orders, payment references, delivery details and transaction records, and will share payment details with the payment provider. This policy will be updated before those features are switched on.
