What SokoHai is — and is not

SokoHai is an online marketplace where people in Tanzania list and find products, services and transport providers and contact each other. Today SokoHai:

FeatureStatus
Accounts, browsing, search, chat, comments and reviewsAvailable
Free listings for products, services and transportAvailable
Reporting listings, chats, stories and live sessions; moderationAvailable
Business and point-of-sale toolsAvailable (some actions need the server functions below)

In-app checkout and orders, SokoPay escrow, wallet, card / mobile-money paymentsNot available yet

Delivery booking, driver assignment and transport paymentsNot available yet
