Community guidelines

How to behave on SokoHai: in listings, chat, comments, reviews, stories and live sessions.

Be honest

  • No fraud, scams, fake listings, fake reviews or fake accounts.
  • Do not impersonate another person, business or SokoHai staff.
  • Do not manipulate prices, ratings or search results.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech or bullying.
  • No sexual content involving minors, and no adult sexual content.
  • No content that promotes violence or self-harm.

Keep it safe

  • Do not share other people’s personal information without permission.
  • Do not ask for or share passwords, PINs, one-time codes or full card numbers.
  • No malware, phishing links or attempts to break the app’s security.
  • No spam, mass messaging or unsolicited advertising in chat.

Live sessions and stories

  • The same rules apply in live video and stories.
  • Show only what you have the right to show, and do not record or stream people who have not agreed.
  • Sellers in live sessions must describe products accurately.

Content that breaks the law

Content that is illegal in Tanzania is not allowed, including prohibited items listed in the Seller policy. LEGAL REVIEW: obligations under the Electronic and Postal Communications (Online Content) Regulations and their amendments

Reporting

Use Ripoti on a listing, or the report options in chat, stories and live sessions. Reports are reviewed by the SokoHai team. Misusing the report system is itself a breach of these guidelines.

What happens when rules are broken

SokoHai may remove content, hide listings, limit features, or suspend or close accounts. Serious cases may be referred to the authorities. You can ask for a decision to be reviewed through Contact Support.
