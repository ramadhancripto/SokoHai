What SokoHai is

SokoHai (“live market”) brings the everyday Tanzanian market online. Sellers, service providers and transport providers publish listings for free. Buyers browse by category and region, search, compare and contact sellers directly through SokoHai chat.

The name “SokoHai” is the platform’s main brand. Other projects are not part of SokoHai unless this page says so.

The problem we want to solve

Many people sell through scattered social-media posts and word of mouth. Buyers struggle to find what is available nearby, to compare prices, and to know whom to trust. Small businesses lack simple tools to show their stock and reach customers.

SokoHai aims to give them one organised, searchable market with clear listings, real seller profiles, reporting and moderation, and tools for small businesses.

Mission, vision and values

Mission: make it easy and safe for anyone in Tanzania to buy, sell and offer services online.

Vision: a trusted digital market that connects buyers, sellers, service providers and transporters across the country.

Values:

  • Honesty: show real listings and real information, and say clearly what is not ready yet.
  • Safety: protect users’ privacy, act on reports, and keep moderation decisions on record.
  • Simplicity: Swahili-first, works on ordinary phones and slow networks.
  • Fairness: free listings, with the same rules for every seller.

For buyers

  • Browse and search listings by category and region, without an account.
  • Create a free account to chat with sellers, save items, follow shops and leave reviews.
  • Report anything suspicious with one tap.

For sellers and service providers

  • Post products, services or transport offers for free, with photos and prices.
  • Manage listings, stock and a Recycle Bin from your dashboard.
  • Choose whether buyers can see your phone number; otherwise they reach you by chat.
  • Use the business and point-of-sale tools to run your shop.

Trust, transparency and security

Public listings never show your exact GPS location or private contact details unless you choose to show your phone number. Private information is protected by database access rules. Reports are reviewed by people, and moderation actions are logged. Read the Privacy Policy and the Community guidelines.
